Testard OS
Based on Alpine Linux

Install Testard OS

A lean server for your homelab or a VPS: keys-only SSH, a firewall and daily security updates, ready from the first boot. It takes about five minutes.

Your account

You log in with this user. Root login is turned off.

At least 8 characters. Used by sudo.
